# Invoice-style inventory UI

The Inventory Management screen now captures the main commercial stock documents through invoice-style dialogs:

- Quotations: customer lookup, product lookup, multiple lines, subtotal, VAT and grand total.
- Purchase Orders: supplier lookup, warehouse/receiving location lookup, product lookup, multiple lines, subtotal, VAT and grand total.
- Goods Receipts: supplier lookup or PO-based receiving, warehouse/storage location lookup, multiple product lines, subtotal, VAT and grand total.
- Sales Orders: customer lookup, warehouse lookup, product lookup, multiple lines, subtotal, VAT and grand total.

Purchase Order receiving fetches the full PO and pre-populates remaining open lines for receiving.
